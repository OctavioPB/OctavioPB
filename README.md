## Positioning

I transform complex business challenges into scalable data products and platforms, from pipeline to decision.

My work sits where deep engineering meets business strategy. I cover the full data value chain: data engineering, data science, AI development, and the architecture and strategy that connect them to an executive decision.

Senior Data Science Engineer at **Teradata**. Based in the Tijuana–San Diego corridor, working remotely on the Pacific time zone.

## From Pipeline to Decision

```text
 PIPELINE           MODEL             SEMANTIC LAYER       DECISION

 Kafka · ETL        MLOps · ML        Data marts           Executive dashboards
```
